# BUILD-2026-09-15-r47 — Narrative layer: HQ, NPCs, dialogue, quests

Round owner: forge + narrative (data) + chip (integration). Date: 2026-09-15.

## What this round did

1. **Headquarters hub** (`game/ui/hq_scene.py`) — the player loads here after death instead of
   straight to the end screen. WASD movement, E to interact, ESC to menu. Camera follows player.
   Renders rooms (6), NPCs (5), HUD with quest tracker, dialogue system, room transitions.

2. **Dialogue system** (`game/systems/dialogue.py`) — `DialogueTree` with sequential lines,
   NPC-gated dialogues loaded from `game/data/dialogue/<id>.json`. Fallback default dialogue.

3. **Quest tracker** (`game/systems/quest_tracker.py`) — accepts quests, tracks objectives,
   completion rewards, floor-based progression. Active quests show on HUD.

4. **HQ system** (`game/systems/hq.py`) — loads NPC/room data, manages player position in HQ,
   handles NPC interactions, room transitions.

5. **Narrative data** — 5 NPCs (Eira, Brokk, Mira, The Raven, Lena), 6 HQ rooms, 5 quest chains,
   5-act storyline with a coherent premise: "the Depths are a wound bleeding darkness upward."

6. **Scene integration** — `Game.end_run()` now routes death → HQScene (victory still → EndScene).
   `scene_sweep.py` and `death_run.py` updated to test the new flow.

7. **Loop.py lanes** — narrative bot added to `BOT_LANES` and `OWNER_LANES` in the continuous loop.

## Fixes made

- `DATA_DIR` paths: `game/systems/hq.py` and `quest_tracker.py` had `ROOT / "game/data"` which
  resolved to `game/game/data` (nested). Fixed to `ROOT / "data"`.
- `text_size()` returns `(w, h)` tuple — fixed 3 call sites in `hq_scene.py` that treated it as scalar.
- `DialogueTree.current_line()` had a typo `self.lines[self.lines]` — fixed to `self.lines[self.current]`.
- `death_run.py` and `scene_sweep.py` updated for death→HQ flow.
