# BUILD-2026-09-14-r45 — Tile cohesion, camera zoom, minimap fix, UI audit design

Round owner: forge. Date: 2026-09-14.

## What this round did

1. **Tile cohesion (renderer.py)** — replaced uniform-random per-tile decoration
   with themed clustering per room kind: treasure rooms glitter with coins,
   combat/boss/secret rooms have bones/rubble/blood, shrines/omens have cracked
   wear, corridors are mostly plain. Eliminated the jumbled "bones next to coins
   next to blood" salad.

2. **Minimap frame rewrite (ui_minimap_frame.png)** — the qwen3.6-35b model found
   what the pin couldn't see: the original frame had 10 saturated colors (bright
   purple, teal, orange, red, white, light blue, dark blue, brown) arranged in
   disconnected, jagged clusters, scaled non-uniformly from 32×32 to 208×152.
   The pin said "clean" because its composition was fine; the reasoning model
   spotted the chaos. Replaced with a 2px stone border, transparent center,
   bone corner accents — confirmed clean by VLM audit.

3. **Camera zoom (camera.py + renderer.py)** — added `tile_scale` (1.0 default,
   2.0 = closer, half tiles visible), `scaled_tile/view()`, `world_to_screen()`.
   Synced into camera follow/clamp/center math and renderer tile drawing.
   Key gotcha learned: entity blits (`_blit_centered`) need image scaling too —
   if you skip it, entities render at native 32px while tiles render at 64px,
   so the player looks half-size on its own floor. Boss sprites now multiply
   by `2.0 * st` to stay proportionally larger when zoomed.

4. **UI audit strategy** — the per-sprite qwen3.6 audit was killed after
   recognizing 28 sprites × 3-6 min each = hours. Correct workflow per the
   `vlm-visual-qa` skill: bulk screen with the pin first (fast), escalate only
   flagged items to the reasoning model. The minimap frame was the confirmed
   bad apple; the rest need pin-based contact-sheet screening first.

5. **Model verdicts**:
   - qwen3.6-35b: UNSCORED on latency. Text speed 5.0 tok/s (slowest ever
     benched on this box — 10× slower than comparable models). Vision questions
     3-6 min each. Brilliant at describing frames and catching pixel-level
     defects. Designated "second opinion on hard cases" — consult when pin and
     numeric gate disagree.
   - /no_think soft switch does NOT make vision prompts fast — image-encoding
     through the 35B MoE dominates, not just thinking tokens.
