## What this round did

1. **Generated title background** — lone adventurer with lantern at the edge of a vast abyss, five descending arcane platforms, giant skull at the bottom, gothic arch composition. FLUX.1-schnell at 1024×576, resized to 1280×720.
2. **Created dedicated title atlas** — `assets/atlas/title.png` + `.json` (1280×720, single frame `background`). Avoids bloating the UI atlas.
3. **Integrated into MenuScene** — `draw()` now blits the title background instead of filling with void color. Falls back to void if atlas missing.
4. **Palette snap fix** — FLUX output has ~921K off-pixel pixels. Snapped to 26-color vaelmoor palette via numpy nearest-color (same fix as r48 NPC/HQ art).

## Lesson learned

**FLUX → palette-lock pipeline**: raw FLUX output is NOT palette-locked. Every generated image needs:
```
raw → pixelize (grid slice) → resize 32x32 → numpy snap to palette → pack atlas
```
The snap step is mandatory. Without it, `tools.art.verify` fails with thousands of off-palette pixels.
