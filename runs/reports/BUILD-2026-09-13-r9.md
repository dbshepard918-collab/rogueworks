### P1.7 — Economy

**Gold sinks (3):**
1. **Chests cost gold to open.** `world.py:_collect()` chest branch: cost = 10 + 5*floor.
   If player.gold < cost, notice "Chest: X gold needed" and chest stays. Otherwise deduct
   gold, spawn 1-3 loot items via `spawn.chest_loot()`. Gold cost scales with floor so
   late-game chests are real decisions.
2. **Shops sell keys (25% chance).** `world.py:_use_shop()`: after paying shop price,
   25% chance to spawn a key pickup (sprite=prop_key) instead of an item. Helps stuck
   players buy their way to the stairs; keys feel valuable.
3. **Shops sell health vials (20% chance).** `world.py:_use_shop()`: 20% chance to spawn
   a `health_vial` consumable item (from items.json: tier 1, effect={max_hp: 40}, value=25,
   sprite=prop_potion_health). Another gold sink that directly competes with item purchases.

**Essence tuning:**
- `spawn.py:drop_loot_for()`: essence drop chance 22% → 28% (non-boss), amount formula
  unchanged (1-2 + tier*0.8, boss x8). Makes essence more plentiful relative to gold,
  so gold is the scarcer resource and the "spend vs hoard" decision hurts.

**Key economy + HUD:**
- `hud.py:_stats_panel()`: key counter added next to gold display (prop_key icon + count),
  so keys are visible as a resource, not hidden.

**Content:**
- `game/data/items.json`: 1 new entry — `health_vial` (consumable, tier 1, effect max_hp:40,
  value 25, sprite prop_potion_health, flavor "Cracked glass, thicker blood...").

### Validator bug fix (tools lane)
- `tools/validate_data.py`: `unique` field schema changed from
  `{"type": "str", "nullable": True}` to `{"type": "str", "nullable": True, "required": False}`.
  The field is an optional P1.6 extension; the validator was incorrectly requiring it on
  all 72 items (64 non-legendary items had no unique field → 64 errors). With the fix,
  only items that declare unique are validated for it.
