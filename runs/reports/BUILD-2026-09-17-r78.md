# BUILD report — 2026-09-17 (r78)

## Round goal

Ship the 5th biome **The Wound** and its final boss **Heart of the Depths** (`the_wound`),
so quest 5 ("Close the Wound") is completable — at "top-tier game material" quality. This is
the last piece of the quest/narrative chain that r76/r77 wired up.

## What changed

1. **Biome** (`game/data/biomes.json`) — appended `the_wound` ("The Wound"), tileset
   `tile_wound`, modifier `wound_pulse`, flesh-themed monster list, dark-red ambient, fog 0.68,
   `boss_theme` music, 3 secret rooms. `biome_for_floor` maps it to floors 21–25 automatically
   (5th biome slot); the final boss floor is 25.

2. **Modifier `wound_pulse`** (`game/systems/biome_mods.py`, `tools/validate_data.py`) — the
   signature mechanic for a *living* dungeon, distinct from the other four:
   - **heartbeat** — every ~5 s the floor itself beats, dealing global damage scaled +1 per 5
     floors (the Depths are alive and angry);
   - **weeping walls** — standing against a flesh wall applies `bleed` (the walls are veins).
   Added to the `modifier` enum and the `_MODULES`/`step()` dispatch.

3. **Flesh monsters + boss** (`game/data/monsters.json`) — 8 flesh minions across tiers 1–4
   (`flesh_spawn`, `wound_maggot`, `vein_lurker`, `heart_thrall`, `pulse_caller`,
   `viscera_brute`, `wound_horror`, `flesh_weaver`) plus `the_wound` (tier-5 boss, 520 hp,
   `bleed` on hit, `shadow` element) with a full 3-phase arc ("The Heart Beats" → "The Wound
   Opens" → "The Depths Scream"), adds, hazards (`blood_pool`/`vein_lash`/`heart_pulse`) and a
   1.8× enrage — mirroring the `forge_colossus` boss template.

4. **Rooms** (`game/data/rooms.json`) — 16 `the_wound` room templates (entrance, 4 combat,
   treasure, shrine, shop, boss, 3 secret, gambling, blacksmith, fountain, omen), flesh-themed
   props (`prop_tentacle`, `prop_jelly`, `prop_roots`).

5. **Tileset art** (`tools/art/gen_tiles_procedural.py`) — added a `wound` theme with dedicated
   organic `_flesh_floor` / `_flesh_wall` generators (mottled tissue + veins, not brick) routed
   through new `_base_floor` / `_base_wall` helpers. Regenerated the atlas: 34 new
   `tile_wound_*` frames, 0 off-palette pixels.

6. **Ambience** (`game/engine/audio.py`) — `the_wound` (and the previously-silent
   `sunken_ossuary`) added to `_BIOME_AMBIENCE`; the_wound loops `boss_theme`.

7. **Quest 5** (`game/data/quests.json`) — `reach_floor` target 20 → 21 (the_wound biome start);
   `kill_boss` target `the_wound` already reconciled in r77.
