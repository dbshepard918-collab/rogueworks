# BUILD-2026-09-15-r57 — Autopilot stuck recovery: reach floor 15+ and win

## What changed

### Problem
After r56, the autopilot reached floor 10 but got STUCK on certain seeds (4, 5) — grinding against geometry with no recovery path. The stuck detection dropped the target but never picked a new one, so the bot stood still until timeout.

### Solution
Added **recovery mode** to `AutoPilotInput`:

1. **`_recovery_target()`** — picks nearest walkable room center (scored by distance to stairs + player), falls back to stairs tile
2. **`_nearest_walkable()`** — finds nearest walkable tile to a given position
3. **Recovery mode flag** — when stuck, bot enters recovery mode and follows the recovery path, ignoring threats until it reaches the target
4. **Exit conditions** — recovery mode exits when:
   - Bot reaches the recovery target (within 2 tiles)
   - Bot makes progress (moved >4px in 45 ticks)
   - After 4 stuck cycles, full reset (clear avoid set)
