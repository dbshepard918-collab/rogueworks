## What changed

1. **game/engine/scenes.py — MenuScene animated title + settings entry.**
   `MenuScene.tick(dt)` increments `title_anim_timer` cycling `title_anim_frame` (8 frames).
   `MenuScene.draw()` calls `menus_mod.draw_title_animated()` with sine-wave `title_pulse`
   for lantern glow effect. `"settings"` entry added to MenuScene rebuild, wired in
   `handle_event` to push SettingsScene. Pause menu already had settings entry.

2. **game/ui/menus.py — draw_title_animated() + ListMenu class.**
   Renders title text with pulsing colour and cycles `ui_title_frame_N` sprite from the
   `ui` atlas. Fallback to static `draw_title()` if art not loaded. Menu list positioned
   at top=380 to make room for animated title.

3. **game/ui/settings.py — categorized settings screen.**
   `SettingsScene` now has `CATEGORIES` dict (video/audio/controls/accessibility),
   `_category_entries()` builds flat cursor list, `_cycle_category()` switches tabs,
   `handle_event()` with 1-4 key switching, `_cycle()` with volume cycling for
   volume/music_volume/sfx_volume settings, `_draw_volume_sliders()` renders audio bars
   in audio category. Tab labels render at top with category separator line.
