## What changed

### Fixed: 11 items strictly dominate another in the same slot+ tier (P0.8)

The P0.8 balance fix (SLAP #88) corrected the *schema mismatch* that made the balance gate vacuous, but left 11 real dominance pairs where one item is strictly better on all 6 stats (armor/crit/damage/luck/max_hp/speed) at the same or lower price than a weaker counterpart. These are design defects — players have no reason to choose the strictly worse item.

Root cause: the dominating items were priced too low relative to their stat advantage. Fixed by raising each dominating item's `value` above the item it dominates, so the player faces a meaningful tradeoff.

**11 dominance pairs fixed:**
- `salt_helm_a` (12→13) > `torn_leather_cap` (12) — same slot, same price
- `lucky_coin` (14→15) > `brine_brew_t` (14) — same slot, same price
- `small_health_draught` (10→12) > `brine_draught_c` (11) and `sand_draught_c` (11)
- `small_shell_p` (9→10) > `pilgrim_bandage` (9) — same slot, same price
- `shell_cap_a` (13→15) > `mussel_plate_a` (14) and `sand_mail_a` (13)
- `iron_mace` (40→46) > `sea_claymore_w` (45) and `coral_halberd_w` (43)
- `coral_vest_a` (210→229) > `magma_cuirass` (228)
- `kraken_elixir_c` (205→211) > `lava_ward_potion` (210)
