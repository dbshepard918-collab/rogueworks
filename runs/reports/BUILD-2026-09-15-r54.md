### Gate 4 — tools.art.verify
**Result: FAIL** — 13 errors, 1 warning.

- `assets/sprites/ui/title_background.png`: 1280×720, not a multiple of the 32px tile grid
- **921,598 off-palette pixels** across 200,270 distinct colours — the entire image is raw FLUX output with no palette snap
- Nearest palette colour for the dominant dark tones is `void` (#0b0a10), distances 18-21 units away
- `assets/atlas/title.json`: frame `background` is 1280×720, not a tile-grid multiple

**Root cause:** `title_background.png` was generated via FLUX.1-schnell in r49 and the palette-snap fix documented in r49 ("Lesson learned: raw FLUX output is NOT palette-locked") apparently did not persist or was lost. The file in its current state is the raw, unsnapped FLUX output — exactly the defect r49 was meant to fix.
