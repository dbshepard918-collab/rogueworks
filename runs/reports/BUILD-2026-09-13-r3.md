## P0: Broken main (fixed first)

`game.main --headless` crashed on every seed with `AttributeError: 'dict' object has no
attribute 'x'` at `game/ui/minimap.py:39`. The minimap iterates `level.rooms` and accesses
`room.x`, `room.kind`, `room.revealed` as attributes — but `procgen.generate()` was passing
raw dicts to `Level(...)`. The `Room` class (with `__getattr__` for attribute access) existed
but was never instantiated.

**Fix:** In `procgen.generate()`, wrap room dicts in `Room` objects via `_room_from_data()`
before constructing `Level`. Map `spawn_room`/`stairs_room` to their `Room` counterparts by
index. Added `.get()` method to `Room` for dict-style consumers.

**Before:** 4 gates red (3 headless + selftest), exit 1.
**After:** 7 gates green, exit 0.
