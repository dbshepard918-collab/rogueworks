## Bug found

`world._collect()` line 510: `tax = int(gross * getattr(world, "gold_tax", 0.0))` referenced the module-level `world` object instead of `self`. This meant the gold tax feature (added in P1.5) was never actually applied — players kept 100% of gold drops. Fixed to `self.gold_tax`.

Also: `world.__init__` had `self.shrine_active_effects = []` listed twice (once as `shrine_active_effects`, once as `shrine_temp_effects`). Deduped to a single assignment.
