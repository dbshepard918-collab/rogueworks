# BUILD-2026-09-15-r55 — Player hazard feedback: VFX + camera shake

## What changed

### Problem
r54 animated hazard tiles (burning/water/torch) but the player character got no visual feedback — standing on a flickering burning tile looked the same as standing on safe stone. Silent hazard damage = "why am I dying?"

### Solution

**`_step_player_hazards(dt)` in `game/systems/world.py`:**
- Called every tick from `World.step()` after run_state check
- Cooldown-gated (one event per 0.7s burning / 0.8s water) — no RNG
- Burning tile: spawn `vfx_flame` sprite burst at player feet + camera shake (3.0) + hit_flash (0.08s)
- Water tile: spawn `vfx_ripple` sprite burst at player feet (no shake — water isn't violent)
- Cosmetic only — the real DOT lives in `biome_mods.py`
